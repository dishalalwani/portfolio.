02 / The room

Brick you can touch. Cloth overhead.

A brick wall hung with framed prints, rattan lamps glowing under a ceiling of draped cloth, and a bar that faces the fire. There’s rooftop seating too, and guests say it looks its best around sunset. The whole place was built from bare concrete, one phase at a time.
